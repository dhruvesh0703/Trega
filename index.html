Trega: Hyperlocal Marketplace

Welcome to Trega, the premier enterprise-grade hyperlocal second-hand and peer-to-peer marketplace tailored specifically for your university and neighborhood zones.

At Trega, we prioritize trust and security. Our platform features integrated KYC verification, allowing students and residents to verify their identities using Student IDs or Aadhaar cards. This ensures that every transaction is conducted with verified members of the community, fostering a safe environment for buying and selling.

Our marketplace includes a robust escrow ledger checkout system, ensuring that payments are secure until both the buyer and seller are satisfied with the transaction. Whether you are looking for electronics, textbooks, furniture, or services, our geofencing and radius filtering ensure you find what you need within your local vicinity.

Discover real-time listings, negotiate prices using our "Make an Offer" feature, and engage with the community through public Q&A on listings. Join Trega today and experience a seamless, secure, and localized peer-to-peer trading experience in your city.

Features include:

  • Trust Verification (KYC) for all users.
  • Escrow checkout using secure payment gateways like UPI and Net Banking.
  • Geofenced product discovery tailored for local neighborhoods.
  • Public Q&A and active dispute management for buyer protection.
